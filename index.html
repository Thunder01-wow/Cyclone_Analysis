About CycloneAI • Smart India Hackathon 2026

Operational Challenge

Traditional tropical cyclone intensity estimation relies heavily on manual Dvorak technique visual analysis of satellite imagery, which introduces human subjectivity and up to 3-hour operational advisory delays during rapidly evolving oceanic storms.

CycloneAI Solution

CycloneAI automates satellite telemetry analysis by leveraging a deep residual convolutional neural network calibrated directly on INSAT-3D/3DR geostationary satellite channels, providing automated sub-minute intensity estimation and numerical-deep hybrid trajectory forecasting.

Current Prototype Scope (Delivered)
  • ✅ Automated wind speed regression from satellite IR tiles
  • ✅ RSMC New Delhi IMD Cyclone Scale classification
  • ✅ 48-Hour Trajectory Prognosis & GIS Uncertainty Cone Map
  • ✅ Kaplan-DeMaria Rapid Intensification (RI) Risk index
  • ✅ FastAPI backend integration layer with JSON/GeoJSON payload export
Future Roadmap
  • 🚀 Multi-spectral sensor fusion (Microwave + Visible + Water Vapor)
  • 🚀 3D Convective eyewall structure reconstruction
  • 🚀 Real-time IMD GTS automated bulletin generation
